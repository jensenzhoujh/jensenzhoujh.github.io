Jensen (Jinghao) Zhou

Jensen (Jinghao) Zhou

jensen.zhoujh@gmail.com


I am a third-year Ph.D. student at University of Oxford supervised by Prof. Christian Rupprecht and Prof. Philip Torr. I am a part of both the Visual Geometry Group as well as Torr Vision Group.

I like building multi-modal generative models and (self-)supervised generalist models, with a string of fun research experiences at Meta (2025), Stability AI (2024), Google (2023), Microsoft (2022), ByteDance (2021), JHU (2021), and SenseTime (2020).

I find joy in telling stories—of my research, crafts, reflections, and becoming. If you're curious to read a few, begin here.
